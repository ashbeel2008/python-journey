## When you're stuck

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| No replies | Your first line isn't specific. Name a real moment from their content. |
| Replies but no calls | You're selling "editing" instead of a result. Use the offer sentence. |
| Calls but no clients | You're talking to people with no money. Qualify harder. |
| Editing too slow | Cut the clip length, not the quality. |
| Overwhelmed | Do only Section 5 of `03-DAY-1-SETUP.md`. Nothing else. |
| Premiere lags | Playback 1/8, close Chrome, clear media cache, short clips. |
