# 03 — Outreach Scripts

The goal of outreach is **not** to sell in the first message. It's to earn a reply.
Replies become calls. Calls become clients.

---

## Where to find prospects (pick 2 sources, not 5)

| Source | How to use it | Best for |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| Instagram / TikTok search | Search `[niche] + [your city]`, look at recently active accounts with weak video | Creators, local businesses |
| Google Maps | `[niche] in [city]`, check their website/socials for video | Local businesses |
| Apple Podcasts / Spotify / Listen Notes | Search your niche, find shows with no clips on social | Podcasters |
| YouTube | Search niche, filter "this month," look for inconsistent uploads | YouTubers |
| Facebook groups | Local business / entrepreneur groups; be helpful, don't spam | Local businesses |

**Qualify before you message:**
- Active in the last 60 days
- Already posts some video (so they value it)
- You can find a real contact name or handle
- You can name one specific thing they could improve

---

## Daily routine (30 minutes of the hour)

1. **10 min:** find 10 qualified prospects, add to tracker.
2. **15 min:** send 10 personalized messages.
3. **5 min:** follow up on messages from 3 days ago.

**10 a day × 5 days = 50/week.** At a 10–20% reply rate, that's 5–10 conversations.
At a 20–30% close rate on calls, that's 1–3 clients. That's the whole game.

---

## Script 1 — Instagram DM (creator / podcaster)

Personalize line 1. Never copy-paste it.

> Hey [NAME] — the [specific episode/video] on [topic] was genuinely good, especially the part
> about [detail]. I noticed you're posting long-form but not cutting it into Reels.
>
> I do exactly that: one episode → 8–10 vertical clips with captions, hooks, and a consistent look.
> I made a 20-second sample from your [episode] so you can see the style — want me to send it?

Why it works: specific compliment → clear observation → concrete offer → tiny ask.

---

## Script 2 — Email to a local business

Subject: `Quick idea for [Business]'s Instagram`

> Hi [NAME],
>
> I'm [YOUR NAME], a video editor in [CITY]. I looked at [Business]'s Instagram — the [recent
> post/offer] is strong, but there's no video showing it.
>
> I help [niche] businesses turn one shoot into 4–8 short Reels a month: hooks, captions, and a
> consistent look, delivered in 3 days.
>
> If you send me 10 minutes of phone footage, I'll cut one 30-second sample free so you can judge
> the style before paying anything. Worth a try?
>
> [NAME] · [PHONE] · [PORTFOLIO LINK]

---

## Script 3 — Follow-up (send 3 days later, then 7 days later)

> Hey [NAME], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send that sample — no strings.
> If it's not a fit, no worries at all, I'll stop bugging you.

Two follow-ups maximum. Then mark "Nurture" and move on.

---

## Script 4 — Upwork / Freelance proposal (first line only matters)

> You need [specific outcome from their post], not just "an editor."
>
> I'd cut this as: [hook in first 2s] → [3 beats] → [CTA]. I've attached a 30-second sample in
> that style. If you like the pacing, I can deliver [X] videos by [DATE] for [$Y].
>
> Two questions: [smart question about their audience] and [question about footage]?

Rules: reference their actual project, give a plan, give a price, ask a question.

---

## Script 5 — Discovery call (15 minutes)

Ask these, in this order. Shut up and listen.

1. "What are you trying to achieve with video over the next 3 months?"
2. "Who exactly is watching — describe them."
3. "What have you tried before, and what didn't work?"
4. "How many videos a month do you actually want?"
5. "Who films and who approves the final cut?"
6. "What's your budget range for this?"
7. "If we start, when do you need the first video?"

Then: "Based on that, here's what I'd recommend..." → send contract same day.

---

## Objection handling

**"It's too expensive."**
> "Totally fair. The [Starter] package is [$X] and covers [fewer videos]. We can start there and
> scale up when you see results. Which fits your budget better?"

**"Can you do a free test?"**
> "I don't do full free edits, but I'll cut a 20-second watermarked sample from your footage so
> you can judge the style. If you like it, we start with 50% deposit."

**"We already have an editor."**
> "Good — that means you value video. I'm not asking you to switch. If they're ever overloaded or
> you need a faster turnaround, keep me as a backup. Can I send a sample?"

**"Send me your portfolio."**
> "Here's the link: [LINK]. The [specific piece] is closest to what you'd need. Want me to make a
> 20-second sample using your footage so it's not generic?"

**"Let me think about it."**
> "Of course. What's the one thing you'd need to be sure about? I can answer it now or send
> something specific."

**"We'll pay you after it performs."**
> "I work on a flat fee, not performance — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. That keeps the timeline
> predictable for both of us."

---

## The close (after the call)

> "Great — I'll send the agreement and invoice today. Once the 50% deposit lands, I'll start and
> you'll have the first cut by [DATE]."

Send contract + invoice **within 2 hours**. Speed closes deals.
