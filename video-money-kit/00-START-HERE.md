# Money Kit — Video Editing for a Complete Beginner

Built for **one hour a day**, starting from zero. No paid software required.

Read this file once. Then live inside `01` → `06`.

> **Gaming is your field? Read `gaming/00-YOUR-GAMING-PATH.md` first.**
> It replaces the niche and pricing files with a gaming-specific track (and accounts for your
> laptop's limits). The contract, tracker, invoice, and delivery checklist below still apply.

---

## The rule that decides whether this works

> **You are not selling editing. You are selling a result to a specific person.**

"Video editor" is a commodity. "I make 60-second Reels for gym owners that put their free-trial
offer in front of locals" is a business.

One niche. One offer. One type of client. Everything in this kit assumes that.

---

## 14-day plan, 1 hour/day

| Day | Task (~60 min) | Output |
|-----|----------------|--------|
| 1 | Pick your niche (`01`) | One sentence: who + result |
| 2 | Set starter price + packages (`02`) | 3 packages written down |
| 3 | Make 1 spec edit from free footage | Proof piece #1 |
| 4 | Make 2 more spec edits | Proof pieces #2–3 |
| 5 | Build a 1-page portfolio (Canva or Google Doc is fine) | A link you can send |
| 6 | Build a list of 50 prospects (`03`) | 50 names in the tracker |
| 7 | Send 10 outreach messages | 10 sent |
| 8 | Send 10 + follow up on Day 7 | 20 total touches |
| 9 | Send 10 + follow up | 30 total touches |
| 10 | Send 10 + follow up | 40 total touches |
| 11 | Handle replies + calls (`03`) | 1–3 conversations |
| 12 | Send contract + invoice (`04`, `05`, `invoice-template.html`) | First paid deal |
| 13 | Deliver using checklist (`06`) | First delivery |
| 14 | Ask for testimonial + referral | Review + next lead |

You will not get rich in 14 days. You **can** get your first paid client, which is the only thing
that matters right now. Money follows proof, proof follows reps.

---

## Non-negotiables

1. **One niche for 30 days.** Don't switch because it's slow.
2. **Charge from client #1.** Even $20. Free work is only ever a *spec sample you chose*, never
   "work for exposure."
3. **Two revisions max**, written into the scope.
4. **50% upfront** before you start editing.
5. **10 outreach messages a day** on outreach days. Volume beats perfection.
6. **Use the tools you already have.** Don't buy plugins, LUTs, or courses before your first paid client.
