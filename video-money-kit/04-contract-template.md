# 04 — Contract Template

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting point for small freelance jobs.
> If a client is large or the deal is worth a lot, have a local lawyer check it. Laws on
> contracts, taxes, and invoicing vary by country.

Replace everything in `[BRACKETS]`. Delete what doesn't apply. Send as a PDF.

---

# VIDEO EDITING SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Date:** [DATE]

**Between:**
- **Editor:** [YOUR FULL NAME / BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS], [EMAIL], [PHONE] ("Editor")
- **Client:** [CLIENT NAME / BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS], [EMAIL] ("Client")

---

## 1. Scope of work

Editor will produce:

| Deliverable | Quantity | Length | Format |
|-------------|----------|--------|--------|
| [e.g., short-form videos] | [4] | [30–60s] | [1080×1920 MP4] |
| [e.g., captions] | [included] | — | [burned-in] |

**Included:** editing, captions, licensed music, [basic color], [2] revision rounds.

**Not included:** raw project files, unlimited revisions, shooting, paid stock assets, posting to
Client's accounts, scriptwriting from scratch.

**Deadline:** first cut by **[DATE]**, final delivery by **[DATE]**.

---

## 2. What the Client provides

- All raw footage, logos, fonts, and brand assets
- Written direction or a reference video before editing begins
- One named person with authority to approve the final cut
- Feedback within **[48] hours** of receiving each cut

Delays in providing these move the deadline by the same number of days.

---

## 3. Revisions

- **[2] rounds** of revisions per deliverable are included.
- A "round" means one consolidated set of written notes.
- Additional rounds are billed at **[$X] per round**.
- Revisions requested more than **7 days** after delivery are treated as new work.

---

## 4. Fees and payment

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Total project fee | **$[AMOUNT]** |
| Deposit (50%) due before work starts | **$[AMOUNT]** |
| Balance (50%) due on approval, before final files | **$[AMOUNT]** |

- Payment method: [BANK / UPI / PAYPAL / WISE]
- Invoices are due within **[7] days**.
- Work pauses if an invoice is more than **7 days** late.
- A **5% late fee** applies after 14 days.
- Rush work (under 48 hours) carries a **30% surcharge**.
- Prices are in **[USD]**; bank fees are paid by the Client.

---

## 5. Ownership

- On receipt of **full payment**, Client owns the final delivered videos and may use them
  worldwide, in any medium, without further payment.
- Until full payment, all files remain the property of the Editor.
- **Raw project files are not included** unless purchased separately for **[$X]**.
- Editor retains the right to display the work in a portfolio and on social media unless Client
  requests otherwise **in writing before delivery**.

---

## 6. Cancellation

- If Client cancels before work begins: deposit is refunded minus a **[$X] admin fee**.
- If Client cancels after work begins: the **deposit is non-refundable**.
- If Editor cannot complete the work: all payments for undelivered work are refunded.

---

## 7. Independent contractor

Editor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Editor is responsible for their own taxes,
equipment, and insurance.

---

## 8. Limitation

Editor is not liable for claims, strikes, or account bans arising from Client's content, music
choices, or platform policies. Client is responsible for having rights to all footage and music
they supply.

---

## 9. Entire agreement

This is the entire agreement between the parties. Changes must be in writing (email is fine) and
agreed by both sides.

## How to send it

1. Duplicate this file per client.
2. Fill every bracket. No `[BRACKETS]` left in the sent version.
3. Export to PDF (open in Word/Google Docs → Download as PDF).
4. Send with the invoice in the same message.
5. Ask them to reply "agreed" or sign and return — an email saying "agreed" is usually enough
   for small jobs.
