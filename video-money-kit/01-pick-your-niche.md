# 01 — Pick Your Niche

## Why this matters more than your editing skill

A generalist beginner competes with millions of people on Fiverr on price.
A specialist competes with almost nobody, because the client feels understood.

Clients don't buy "editing." They buy:

- "Someone who understands gym marketing."
- "Someone who can turn my podcast into clips without me explaining my brand."
- "Someone who makes my listings look expensive."

---

## The three starter niches (pick ONE)

### A. Podcast / creator clipping — **best for 1 hour/day**

You take long-form content that already exists and cut short vertical clips.

- **Who:** podcasters, coaches, newsletter writers with a YouTube channel, small YouTubers.
- **Why it's easiest:** the footage already exists. No shooting. No client on set. Repeatable.
- **Where to find them:** Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Listen Notes, YouTube search for your topic +
  "podcast," Instagram accounts that post long videos but no Reels.
- **Typical first price:** $25–50 per clip, or $150–250 for a 10-clip pack.

### B. Local businesses (gyms, salons, dentists, real estate)

- **Who:** businesses that already post video but badly, or not at all.
- **Why it pays more:** owners have budget and hate doing it themselves.
- **Catch:** sometimes you need to shoot. You can start by editing footage they send you.
- **Where to find them:** Google Maps for your city + niche, Instagram location tags, Facebook
  local business groups.
- **Typical first price:** $150–300 per promo video, $400–800/month retainer.

### C. YouTubers / course creators

- **Who:** channels with 1k–100k subscribers posting inconsistently.
- **Why it's good:** recurring monthly work once you're in.
- **Catch:** more competitive, higher expectations, longer edits.
- **Typical first price:** $80–200 per long-form edit, $600–1,500/month retainer.

## Choose in 10 minutes

Score each niche 1–3 on these three questions. Highest total wins.

| Question | A. Clipping | B. Local | C. YouTube |
|----------|-------------|----------|------------|
| Can I find 50 of these people this week? | | | |
| Do they already publish video (so they value it)? | | | |
| Can I reach the decision-maker directly? | | | |

**Default if you're unsure: A (podcast/creator clipping).** Fastest feedback loop, lowest friction,
and the skills transfer to everything else.

---

## Write your one-sentence offer

Fill this in. Keep it under 20 words.

> I help **[WHO]** **[RESULT]** by **[WHAT]**, so they can **[OUTCOME]**.

Examples:

- "I help podcasters turn one episode into 10 scroll-stopping Reels, so they grow without
  recording more."
- "I help gym owners in [CITY] get their free-trial offer in front of locals with weekly Reels,
  so they stop relying on walk-ins."
- "I help real estate agents make listings look premium with 60-second walkthrough videos, so
  they get more viewing requests."

**Write yours here:**

> I help ____________________________________

## Red flags — say no to these

- "We need video for everything" → no clear scope, endless revisions.
- Hasn't posted in 6 months → doesn't value video enough to pay.
- Agency subcontracting at $10/video with "more work later" → you're the cheap labor.
- "Pay you after the campaign performs" → you're taking their risk for free.
- Asks for a free full edit "to test you" → offer a 20-second watermarked sample instead.

---

## Checkpoint

You are done with this file when you can say your offer out loud in one breath and name **three
specific places** you'll find 50 prospects.
