# 06 — Delivery Checklist

Never send a file without running this list. Most beginners lose clients to sloppy delivery,
not bad editing.

---

## Before you export

- [ ] **Hook in the first 1–3 seconds.** No logo intro. No slow fade.
- [ ] **Audio levels:** voice peaks around **-6 to -12 dB**; music sits **-18 to -24 dB** under
      voice. Nothing clipping.
- [ ] **Captions are accurate.** Read every line. Fix names, brands, numbers.
- [ ] **Safe zones:** no text under the top 15% or bottom 20% (platform UI covers it).
- [ ] **Spelling:** client name, business name, offer details, prices, dates — all correct.
- [ ] **Brand colors/fonts** match what the client gave you.
- [ ] **No watermarks** from stock sites or trial software.
- [ ] **Watch it once on your phone at full size**, then once **muted**. If it doesn't make sense
      muted, fix the visuals.

---

## Export settings

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Container | MP4 (H.264) |
| Resolution | 1080 × 1920 (vertical) or 1920 × 1080 (horizontal) |
| Frame rate | Match source (usually 30 or 60 fps) |
| Bitrate | 10–15 Mbps for 1080p |
| Audio | AAC, 320 kbps, 48 kHz |
| Color | Rec.709 / sRGB |

Export a **watermarked preview** for approval, then the clean file after final payment.

---

## File naming

```
ClientName_Project_YYYYMMDD_v1.mp4
ClientName_Project_YYYYMMDD_v1_FINAL.mp4
```

No `finalfinal_v3_REAL.mov`. Ever.

---

## Delivery message template

> Hey [NAME] — first cut is ready: [LINK]
>
> What's in it: [X] videos, captions burned in, [notes].
>
> Please send all notes in one message by [DATE] so I can do one revision pass. After that, the
> next round is [$X] per the agreement.
>
> If it looks good, reply "approved" and I'll send the final files + invoice for the balance.

---

## Revision handling

- Collect feedback in **one** message. Don't chase scattered DMs.
- Repeat back: "So: change the hook, swap the music, cut 5 seconds at the end. Anything else?"
- Do the pass. Send it. Say: "That's revision round 1 of 2 used."
- Track rounds in the tracker notes so nobody "forgets."

---

## After delivery (this is where repeat business comes from)

1. Send the invoice the same day.
2. One week later: "How did the videos perform?" (Ask for a number — views, saves, DMs.)
3. Ask for a testimonial using the result: *"Would you be okay if I quoted that?"*
4. Ask: "Do you know one other [niche] person who'd want the same?"
5. Add them to a monthly check-in list.

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Preview approved
- [ ] Payment cleared
- [ ] Final files delivered with clean names
- [ ] Invoice sent
- [ ] Testimonial requested 7 days later
