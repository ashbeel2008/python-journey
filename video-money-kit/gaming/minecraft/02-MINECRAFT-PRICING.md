## Scope boundaries

**Included:**
- [ ] Exact number of videos
- [ ] Length range
- [ ] Format (1080×1920 or 1920×1080 MP4)
- [ ] Captions
- [ ] Sound effects + licensed music
- [ ] **2 revision rounds**
- [ ] Turnaround
- [ ] Google Drive delivery

**Not included:**
- Raw project files (Premiere)
- Unlimited revisions
- Building the thing being filmed (you edit; you don't build their server)
- Paid shaders, mods, or stock assets
- Posting to their accounts
- Thumbnails (unless packaged)

---

## Payment terms

1. **50% deposit** before editing starts.
2. **50% on delivery**, before unwatermarked files.
3. Monthly retainers paid **upfront**.
4. Late 7 days → work pauses. Late 14 days → 5% fee.
5. Watermark previews until balance clears.
6. Rush fee **+30%** for under-48-hour turnaround.
7. **No revenue share. No staff ranks. No "exposure."**

---

## The price ladder

1. Two or three clients at starter prices.
2. Get a testimonial with a **result** ("first Short hit 80k", "trailer brought 200 joins").
3. Raise **20–30%**.
4. Repeat until you lose ~1 in 3 deals — that's your market rate.
5. Never lower your price. Offer a smaller package.
