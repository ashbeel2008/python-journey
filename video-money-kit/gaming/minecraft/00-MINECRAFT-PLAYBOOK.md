# Minecraft Playbook

You play Minecraft. That means you can **generate your own footage on the laptop you already
have** — no camera, no client, no travel. That is a bigger advantage than it sounds.

Minecraft is also one of the few games where the audience watches **calm, story-driven, long**
content as much as fast clips. That gives you two markets instead of one.

**Your first three months: Shorts, build showcases, and server trailers.** They're short, they
export fast, and they're what your laptop can actually handle.

## Who pays in Minecraft

**Target:**
- **Minecraft YouTubers, 1k–100k subs** — posting survival/100 Days/SMP content, inconsistent
  uploads, no Shorts. They need editors badly.
- **Minecraft streamers, 50–1,500 viewers** — need clips for TikTok/Shorts.
- **Minecraft server owners** — need trailers, promos, and update videos. **Highest pay per hour.**
- **Faceless Minecraft Shorts channels** — post daily, always need editors. Low pay ($10–25/video)
  but great for fast reps and testimonials.

**Avoid:**
- "Edit for us and you get a staff rank / you can join the SMP." That's payment in pixels.
- Revenue share on a server's donations. It's almost always $0.
- 12-year-old clients with no income. Be kind; don't work for free.
- Content farms offering $5 per video forever. Use them for 2 reps max, then move up.

## Your 3 portfolio pieces

Make these. One per evening. Bad first drafts are fine — you're learning the workflow.

### 1. Funny moment Short (15–30s)
Something dumb happens in your world → caption it → sound effects → zoom punch → end on the
reaction.
**Skills:** hook, captions, comedic timing, sfx.

### 2. Build showcase (30–60s)
Build a small house, tower, or base. Film smooth camera pans (spectator mode or slow walk).
Add calm music, a title, and 2–3 text callouts.
**Skills:** pacing, music sync, text design, restraint.

### 3. Server trailer spec (60s)
Pick a **small** public Minecraft server (search "Minecraft server list" — pick one with under
500 players). Make a 60-second trailer using their screenshots/clips or your own visit.
Send it to the owner with a short pitch.
**Skills:** storytelling, structure, and it doubles as outreach.

> Use your own footage. If you clip someone else's video for a portfolio piece, ask permission
> first — most small creators say yes, and it starts a conversation.

## Your recording + editing stack

**Record:** Lunar Client **Rewind**. **Edit:** **Premiere Pro 2019**.

Premiere 2019 officially wants 8 GB RAM; you have 6 GB. It runs, but only if you follow
`04-PREMIERE-LOW-END.md`. That file is not optional — it's the difference between "my laptop can
edit" and "Premiere freezes every 10 seconds."

### Minecraft settings (while recording)
- Render distance: **6–8 chunks**
- Graphics: **Fast**, Particles **Minimal**, Smooth Lighting **Off**
- Max framerate **60**, VSync **Off**
- Window: **1280×720**

### Rewind settings
- Record with **Shift + R**; enable **Shadow Rewind** for "save the last 30 seconds"
- Turn on **Record Microphone** + **Record System Audio**
- Bind a **marker** key — mark moments while playing so you don't scrub later
- Export as **MP4 / H.264**, **1920×1080** (or 1280×720 if slow), **30 fps**
- Export only the section you need — clips under 3 minutes

### Storage rules
- Keep **20 GB free** at all times.
- Delete Rewind exports after the Premiere export is done.
- Premiere's media cache eats disk — clear it weekly (see `04-PREMIERE-LOW-END.md`).

## 30-day Minecraft skill ladder

### Week 1 — The Minecraft look
- Text styles that feel Minecraft-y (blocky fonts, drop shadows)
- Sound effects: pop, click, villager "hmm", note block, explosion
- Captions: bold, high-contrast, 2–4 words at a time
- **Output:** 2 funny-moment Shorts

### Week 2 — Story and pacing
- How to compress 10 minutes into 30 seconds
- Setup → tension → payoff in under 45 seconds
- Music choice (calm vs. tense vs. comedic)
- **Output:** 1 build showcase

### Week 3 — Minecraft-specific structure
- "100 Days in 60 Seconds" style montage
- Day counters, progress wipes, before/after reveals
- Server trailer structure: hook → world → features → CTA
- **Output:** 1 server trailer + 1 montage

### Week 4 — Batch and brand
- Build a reusable Premiere project template
- Consistent intro/outro, fonts, colors
- Thumbnails that match Minecraft YouTube style
- **Output:** a 5-clip portfolio pack
