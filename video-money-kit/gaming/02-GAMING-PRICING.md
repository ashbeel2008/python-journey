# Gaming Pricing and Packages

Starting points in **USD** for a beginner with 0–3 testimonials. Convert for local clients.
International gaming clients pay in USD.

> These are intentionally low enough to get a yes, high enough that you're not free labor.
> They are not guarantees.

---

## Starting prices

| Service | Beginner | After ~5 happy clients |
|---------|----------|------------------------|
| 1 vertical clip (15–60s, captions) | $15–25 | $30–50 |
| 10-clip pack (one VOD) | $120–200 | $250–400 |
| Weekly retainer (8–10 clips/week) | $250–400/mo | $500–900/mo |
| YouTube gaming edit (8–15 min) | $100–200 | $250–500 |
| Thumbnail / cover frame | $10–20 | $25–40 |
| Montage (2–4 min) | $150–300 | $400–800 |

**Quote per project or package. Never per hour.** Per-hour punishes you for getting fast.

## Packages (use these exact three)

### Clip Starter — $180/month
- 8 vertical clips (2/week)
- Captions, hooks, sound design
- 1 revision round per clip
- 3-day turnaround per batch
- *Best for: first-time clients testing you*

### Clip Growth — $420/month  ← push this one
- 20 vertical clips (5/week)
- Captions, hooks, sound design, consistent branding
- 2 revision rounds per clip
- 48-hour turnaround
- Monthly "best performing clip" note
- *Best for: streamers posting daily*

### Channel Partner — $700/month
- 20 vertical clips + 2 YouTube edits
- Thumbnails included
- 2 revision rounds
- 48-hour turnaround
- Monthly 20-minute strategy call
- *Best for: streamers with a YouTube channel too*

**Always show three options.** Most pick the middle. Make the middle the one you want to sell.

## Scope boundaries (put these in every deal)

**Included:**
- [ ] Exact number of clips
- [ ] Length range (15–60s)
- [ ] Format: 1080×1920 vertical MP4
- [ ] Captions: burned-in
- [ ] Sound effects + licensed music
- [ ] **2 revision rounds**
- [ ] Turnaround time
- [ ] Delivery via Google Drive link

**Not included (say it out loud):**
- Raw project files (Premiere)
- Unlimited revisions
- Downloading/archiving their entire VOD library
- Posting to their accounts
- Paid stock music or footage
- Thumbnails (unless in the package)

## Payment terms

1. **50% deposit** before you start. Non-negotiable.
2. **50% on delivery**, before unwatermarked files are sent.
3. Retainers: paid **upfront monthly**, before the month starts.
4. Late 7 days → work pauses. Late 14 days → 5% fee (in the contract).
5. Watermark previews until the balance clears.
6. **Rush fee +30%** for under-48-hour turnaround.
7. **No revenue share. No "pay when it performs." No free month to prove yourself.**

---

## The price ladder

1. Land 2–3 clients at starter prices.
2. Get a testimonial with a **result** ("clips did 40k views", "got 200 new followers").
3. **Raise 20–30%.**
4. Repeat until you lose roughly 1 in 3 deals. That's your market rate.
5. Never lower your price to win. Offer a smaller package instead.

Example over a year: $20/clip → $30 → $40 → $55 → $75.

---

## What to say when they ask "how much?"

> "For 20 clips a month it's [$420], paid monthly. That includes captions, hooks, sound design,
> two revision rounds, and 48-hour turnaround. If you want to start smaller, I do 8 clips for
> [$180]."

Give the number. Then **stop talking**. Let them respond.

---

## Red flags in gaming deals

- Revenue share / "we'll blow up" → decline politely.
- "First month free to prove yourself" → offer a 20-second sample instead.
- "We need 100 clips for $50" → that's $0.50/clip. Decline.
- "Can you also run our TikTok?" → different service, different price. Don't bundle free.
- Payment only after sponsorship lands → you're financing their business. No.
