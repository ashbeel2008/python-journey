# Gaming Outreach — Copy, Paste, Send

You don't need to be a "people person." You need to send 10 of these a day and not take silence
personally. Silence is normal. It is not rejection.

---

## Where gaming clients actually are

| Place | How to find them | Notes |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| **Twitch** | Browse a game category → sort by viewers → look at channels with 50–1,500 viewers | Best source. Check the **About** panel for a business email. Check if they post clips on TikTok/Shorts. |
| **X / Twitter** | Search `[game] streamer`, gaming hashtags, reply guys in the replies | Very active. DMs are open more often than you'd think. |
| **YouTube** | Search `[game] funny moments`, filter "This month", small channels | Look for channels posting long videos but no Shorts. |
| **Discord** | Game servers + streamer communities | Be a human first. Never spam. |
| **Reddit** | r/Twitch, r/streaming, r/NewTubers, r/letsplay | **Read the self-promo rules first.** Some subs ban it. |
| **Fiverr / Upwork** | Search "twitch clip editor", "gaming video editor" | Slower, more competition, but real buyers. |

### Qualify before you message (30 seconds)

- [ ] Active in the last 30 days
- [ ] 50+ concurrent viewers **or** 2k+ subs (they can afford you)
- [ ] Has VODs / long videos to clip
- [ ] Monetized (subs, donations, sponsors) — look for a "Subscribe" button, merch, sponsors
- [ ] No obvious existing clip editor
- [ ] You can name **one specific moment** you'd clip

If you can't name a specific moment, don't message them yet.

## Daily routine (30 of your 60 minutes)

1. **10 min** — find 10 qualified prospects, add to `05-client-tracker.csv`
2. **15 min** — send 10 personalized messages
3. **10 min** — make ONE 20-second sample for the warmest reply
4. **5 min** — follow up on messages from 3 days ago

**10/day × 5 days = 50/week.** At a 10–20% reply rate that's 5–10 conversations. That's the whole
game.

## Script 1 — Twitch business email (best channel)

Find the email in the streamer's **About** panel. Subject line matters.

> **Subject:** clip editor for [CHANNEL] — made you a sample
>
> Hey [NAME],
>
> I watch your [GAME] streams — the [SPECIFIC MOMENT, e.g. "1v4 clutch on Tuesday"] had me
> genuinely laughing. I cut it into a 25-second vertical clip with captions and a hook so you can
> see the style: [LINK]
>
> I do 8–20 clips a month for streamers: hooks, captions, sound design, consistent branding.
> Starter pack is [PRICE]. If the sample isn't your style, no worries — keep it either way.
>
> [YOUR NAME] · [PORTFOLIO LINK] · [PHONE]

## Follow-up (3 days later, then 7 days later)

> Hey [NAME], bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send the sample — no strings. If it's
> not a fit, no worries, I'll stop bugging you.

**Two follow-ups max.** Then mark "Nurture" in the tracker and move on. Desperation smells.

## The gaming-specific traps

### "We'll pay you in revenue share / exposure"

This is the #1 scam aimed at beginner gaming editors. It almost always pays $0.

> "Appreciate the offer, but I work on a flat fee — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. If budget is
> tight, the smallest pack is [$X] and we can start there."

Say it once. If they push, walk away. There are thousands of streamers.

### "We're about to blow up"

Everyone says this. It is not a payment method.

### "Edit my whole channel for free and I'll recommend you"

No. Offer a **20-second watermarked sample** instead. That's the maximum free work.

### "Can you make it go viral?"

> "I can make it watchable and retention-friendly — strong hook, tight pacing, good captions.
> Nobody can guarantee viral. Anyone who does is lying."

Honest answers build trust with serious clients.

## Objection handling

**"You're a beginner."**
> "That's exactly why the sample is free and the price is low. You risk 20 seconds, not a month."

**"Too expensive."**
> "Totally fair. The smallest pack is [$X] — [fewer clips]. We can start there and scale once you
> see the style works."

**"We already have an editor."**
> "Good — that means you value clips. Keep me as a backup for when they're overloaded or you need
> a 24-hour turnaround. Can I send a sample?"

**"Send your portfolio."**
> "Here: [LINK]. The [clip] is closest to your content. Want me to cut a 20-second sample from
> your footage so it's not generic?"

---

## After they say yes

Send the contract + invoice **within 2 hours**. Speed closes deals.

> "Great — sending the agreement and invoice now. Once the 50% deposit lands, I'll start and
> you'll have the first clips by [DATE]."
