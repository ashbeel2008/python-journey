# PYTHON PLAN

**Goal:** by Month 3, build small real tools and earn your first **$50–150**. By Month 6,
**$300–800/month**. Use that to buy better hardware — then restart the video editing plan with a
machine that can actually run it.

**Positioning:** **Python automation + Discord bots** for gaming communities and small businesses.

Why this lane:
- It fits the world you already understand (Minecraft, Discord, servers).
- It runs perfectly on 6 GB RAM — unlike Premiere.
- Real buyers exist: server owners, Discord communities, small businesses drowning in manual work.
- Projects are small enough to finish in a week.

**Time:** 1 hour a day. **20 minutes learning, 40 minutes building.**

---

## The rule that decides this

> **Build more than you watch. 80% code, 20% tutorials.**

You do not "learn Python, then earn." You learn enough to build a small useful thing, sell that
thing, and let the next project teach you more. Watching 200 hours of tutorials produces nothing
you can charge for.

**Mastery is not the first goal.** Competence is. Mastery is what compounds *after* you're getting
paid. At 1 hour/day, 3 months makes you dangerous, not a master — and that's exactly enough to
earn your first money.

**Learn**

**Build four tools**

4. **CSV/Excel report** — reads a spreadsheet, produces a summary

**Done when:** 3 finished scripts in one repo, each with a README and a screenshot.

## Phase 2 — Weeks 5–8: Discord bots + databases

**Learn**

**Build three bots (pick two to finish)**

4. **Finish projects.** One finished beats five abandoned.
5. **Charge from the first client.** Small is fine. Free is not.

## Honest limits

- 1 hour/day for 12 weeks ≈ 84 hours. That's enough to build small paid tools — not to be a
  senior developer.
- Your first money may take **2–4 months**, and it won't be much. That's normal.
- Your laptop is **fine for Python.** The bottleneck is consistency, not hardware.
- "Mastery" is a 5–10 year direction, not a prerequisite. Start earning at competent.
